Questions to Ask About a Cashmere Garment

Compare cashmere garment descriptions, supporting specifications and care instructions without assuming a guaranteed lifespan or a reliable shop-floor quality test.

By Seymour Maison website editorial

A cashmere description can help you decide what to ask a seller. It cannot tell you how an uninspected sweater will perform through years of wear. Start with the particular garment, its complete fibre statement and its care label, then keep any unanswered questions visible.

This editorial was originally published August 3, 2026 and revised October 30, 2026. The revision removes unsupported price and service-life multiples, universal finishing claims and unvalidated rubbing or stretching tests. No garment, fibre sample, supplier record or care method was tested for this page.

Read the complete garment description

Write down the exact product reference and the seller's complete fibre statement. Keep a blend description distinct from a statement that the garment contains only cashmere. If a headline, product description and garment label appear to differ, ask the seller to explain the particular wording before treating them as equivalent.

The US Federal Trade Commission's cashmere guidance discusses fibre identification, content disclosures and care labels. Those are useful categories for organising questions. They do not establish that an individual item has been correctly identified or labelled, and this article makes no product-specific compliance finding.

Ask what a specification actually describes

If a seller provides numbers for fibre diameter, fibre length, yarn construction, knitting or garment weight, ask which product and production stage each number concerns. Is it a specification for the particular item, a range for a collection or a general description of a material? Keep the stated unit, basis and source with the number.

Ask whether supporting documentation is available and what it covers. A detailed description is information to review; it is not independent verification of an entire supply chain. Missing documentation also does not establish a particular cause or prove that the seller has no records.

Keep observations separate from predictions

You can record that a garment feels soft to you or that a visible area appears different from another. Keep the observation attached to the item and the circumstances. A shop impression does not establish fibre identity, remaining service life or the cause of future pilling.

This page provides no rubbing, pulling, light-transmission or recovery test for certifying cashmere quality. It also supplies no universal minimum weight, preferred ply count, seasonal lifetime or replacement interval. If a seller makes a durability comparison, ask what products, conditions and evidence support that particular comparison.

Use the care information for the actual item

Read the attached care instructions and ask the maker or an appropriate cleaner about anything unclear. Keep the garment's construction, trims and stated instructions in that conversation. Do not replace them with a generic claim that every cashmere garment should be hand washed or that one cleaning method always shortens its life.

If a seller recommends a comb, shaver or other treatment, ask whether the advice applies to the particular garment and what instructions accompany it. No cleaning, repair, abrasion or depilling procedure is demonstrated or approved here.

Make the comparison reviewable

A useful comparison note can contain the product reference, full fibre statement, available specifications, care instructions, quoted terms and open questions. Leave a missing value blank or mark it unknown. Do not infer a service-life multiple or cost per wear from an assumed lifetime.

For a separate way to organise the wording of fibre descriptions, see our guide to reading garment fibre statements. For keeping item-specific care information together, see the garment care record. Neither link certifies the composition or condition of a particular purchase.

Reference and scope

FTC: Cachet of Cashmere, read October 30, 2026, supplies limited US fibre-disclosure and care-label context. This page offers no laboratory identification, product ranking, personal suitability assessment, legal determination or professional endorsement.

Common Questions

Can a shop-floor touch test establish cashmere quality?

This page establishes no such test. Record a personal impression separately from fibre identification, a durability prediction or an explanation of pilling.

Does a detailed specification guarantee a longer-lasting sweater?

No lifetime guarantee is established here. Ask which product a specification covers and what evidence supports any separate performance claim.

Should every cashmere garment be washed the same way?

Use the actual garment's care information and ask about unclear instructions. This page supplies no universal washing, dry-cleaning or depilling procedure.

What should I record when comparing two garments?

Keep the product reference, full fibre statement, available specifications, care instructions and unresolved questions together. Do not substitute an assumed lifetime for missing evidence.
